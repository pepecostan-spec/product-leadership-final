# Final Project Presentation

> Module 6 · Final submission
>
> A single deck (Google Slides or PDF) that tells your product-strategy story end to end, built from the deliverable files in this repo.

## What it contains

Five slides, one per deliverable:

1. **Product Strategy**: Playing to Win cascade + OKRs (`01-strategy/`)
2. **Outcome Roadmap**: multi-team roadmap + trade-off memo (`02-roadmap/`)
3. **Team Charter**: What We Own + How We Decide (`03-team-charter/`)
4. **Financial Model**: business case + kill criteria (`04-financial-model/`)
5. **Individual Insights**: friction, learnings, aha (`05-insights/individual-insights.md`)

## How to build it

1. Open the **Final Project Deliverables Template** (in your learning platform / brief) and click **Use Template**.
2. Fill each slide from the matching deliverable file in this repo.
3. _Optional:_ paste your deliverable files into **Gamma**, **Canva**, **Claude**, or **ChatGPT** for a polished deck.
4. Commit the deck to your repo and submit your **repo URL** to the learning platform within **7 days** of your cohort ending.

## Grading (for reference)

Scored 1 to 3 overall: **1, Poor (0 to 49)**, **2, Sufficient (50 to 79)**, **3, Excellent (80 to 100)**, on:

- **Application of concepts**: frameworks and methods from the course
- **Credibility & reasoning**: well-justified choices grounded in course principles
- **Clarity**: clear, logical communication of ideas
- **Strategic thinking**: a clear vision and a defensible bet
