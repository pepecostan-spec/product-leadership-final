# Demo Source Material: Original Nudge Slack Thread

*This is the raw Slack thread that kicked off the entire Engage v2 workspace, reproduced here so the onboarding demo has something real to paste. It wasn't saved as a standalone file when this workspace was first set up — only used inline — so it's captured here now for reuse.*

---

#product-engage — Monday 9:14am

Marcus: Hey team — retention numbers are in. 30-day is at 37%, down from 44% last quarter. We need to figure out what to do. Putting some time on the calendar for Thursday but wanted to start the thread now.

Raj: Was looking at the data last night. The drop is sharpest in users who don't set a savings goal in week 1. Like almost double the churn rate vs users who do.

Lena: That tracks with what I'm hearing in user research. People who connect their account and just see the dashboard don't know what to do next. There's no clear next step after the spending breakdown.

Marcus: So is the problem the home feed? Or is it that we're not surfacing savings goals aggressively enough in onboarding?

Raj: Probably both honestly. But I think the bigger issue is that after week 1 there's nothing pulling people back. The home feed looks exactly the same whether you opened the app yesterday or three weeks ago.

Lena: Yes. And the weekly summary email has like a 22% open rate which is actually decent — but when people click through the app doesn't continue the story the email started. It's jarring.

You: So the hypothesis is: users go passive because the app stops feeling relevant after the first aha moment. We need something that brings them back with a reason that's specific to them, not just generic tips.

Marcus: I like that framing. What would that look like?

Lena: I've been sketching something — a personalized weekly summary screen. Not an email. In-app. Shows your top insight for the week, one actionable nudge based on your actual patterns, and progress toward your savings goal if you have one.

Raj: Technically doable with what we have. We'd need to build the ranking logic for which insight to surface but no new data sources.

Marcus: Ok let's explore this. Can someone write up what we're actually trying to solve before Thursday? I want to make sure we're aligned on the problem before we start designing solutions.
