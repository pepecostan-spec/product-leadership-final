## 2026-07-29 — Metric analysis of retention & A/B test data
Ran real SQL analysis against `Nudge Dataset.xlsx` (500 users, 5 cohort weeks); findings in `data/metric-findings.md`. Confirmed the pre-existing decline is real and had plateaued (day-30 retention 32%→22%, cohort weeks 1–4). Controlled A/B test (cohort week 5, n=50/arm) showed the weekly summary lifted day-7 retention 46%→76% and day-30 retention 22%→36%, with treatment open rate climbing 28%→56% across 4 sends while control stayed flat — strong directional evidence for scaling, caveated by small sample size. Corrected the "almost double" churn claim in `strategy.md` with precise ratios (~1.6x at day 7, ~1.2x at day 30) now that it's checked against real data instead of anecdote.

## 2026-07-29 — Metric diagnosis: decomposed retention into stages
Built a metric tree decomposing 30-day retention into day-1 activation, day-1→7, and day-7→30 stages (`data/metric-diagnosis.md`). Key finding: the weekly summary fixes the day-1→7 transition almost entirely (50.0%→86.4%) but has zero measurable effect on day-7→30 (47.8% vs. 47.4%) — 63% of remaining treatment churn happens in that untouched window, meaning the feature relocated churn more than it eliminated it. Generated 4 ranked hypotheses for why treatment users still churn, each with the data needed to confirm or rule it out. Added this as a new implication for the roadmap in `strategy.md`: day-7-to-30 durability is a distinct, unaddressed problem.

## 2026-07-29 — Results memo drafted for Marcus

Also played skeptical VP of Engineering and raised 3 hard questions, leading with whether n=50/arm is enough to trust the result — which turned out to be the right instinct (see next entry).

## 2026-07-29 — Pressure-tested the pilot; recommendation reversed
Ran formal significance tests and designed a fully powered follow-up (`data/experiment-design.md`). Found the day-30 lift (22%→36%) is **not statistically significant** at n=50/arm (p≈0.12–0.19) — only day-7 (p=0.002) is. Calculated the full test needs ~1,200 users/arm and ~7 weeks (inside the 8-week ceiling; enrollment is fast, the 30-day observation window is the real constraint). Revised recommendation from "scale now" to "run the full test first, keep the pilot live in the meantime" — updated `docs/recommendation-memo.md` and `strategy.md` accordingly. Also specified leading indicators to monitor while the full test runs (day-7 retention, day-1 activation, open-rate trend, opt-out rate, day-7→30 transition, sample ratio mismatch, platform/channel cuts).
